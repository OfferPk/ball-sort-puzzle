How to play

Touch: Tap a tube to select it, then tap another tube to pour. Keyboard: Focus a tube, use the arrow keys to move between tubes (Left/Right within a row; Up/Down to the nearest tube in the adjacent row), then press Enter or Space to select a tube and select another to pour. Accessibility: Each tube's name gives its number, ball colors, and contents; its selected state is exposed through the button's pressed state. Balls can only be poured onto the same color or into an empty tube. Sort every color into its own tube to win! Hint: Shows one recommended legal move without changing the board.
