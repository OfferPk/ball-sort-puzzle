Privacy Policy – Ball Sort Puzzle

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Ball Sort Puzzle ("the app") is developed by OfferPk. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app on Android or play the web version.

Information we collect

The app itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics run by us. Your game progress (current level and settings such as sound on/off) is stored only on your device (in local storage) and is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android app shows ads using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. To serve and measure ads (including personalized ads, where you have consented), AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, app version), and
  • ad interaction data (for example, ad impressions and clicks).

This data is collected and processed by Google under Google's own policies, not by us:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • AdMob / Google advertising policies: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, at any time in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads on newer devices).

The web version at offerpk.github.io does not show ads and does not use cookies.

Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Because we do not collect personal data, we do not store any personal data on our servers.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at https://github.com/OfferPk/ball-sort-puzzle/issues.
