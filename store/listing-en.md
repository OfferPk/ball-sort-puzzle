```
Ball Sort Puzzle is a calm and colorful color sort game. Tap a tube, pour the balls, and sort every color into its own tube. It is easy to learn, fun to master, and a great brain game for a short break.

🧪 HOW TO PLAY
• Tap a tube to pick up the top ball (or balls of the same color).
• Tap another tube to pour them in.
• You can only pour a ball onto the same color, or into an empty tube.
• Each tube holds 4 balls.
• Sort every color into its own tube to win the level!

⭐ FEATURES
• 1000+ levels: endless ball sort puzzles that start easy and slowly get harder.
• Up to 12 colors: bright, clear balls and a clean glass-tube design.
• Every level is solvable: each puzzle is checked by a built-in solver before you play it.
• Play offline: no Wi-Fi needed to play. Enjoy it on the bus, on a plane, or anywhere.
• Undo: 3 free undos in every level to fix a mistake.
• +1 Tube: stuck? Add one extra empty tube once per level.
• No timer and no pressure: think at your own pace.
• Smooth animations, satisfying sounds, and light vibration (you can turn them off in Settings).
• Auto-save: your level and progress are saved on your device.
• Small, simple, and fast: one-hand portrait play on phones.

🧠 A RELAXING BRAIN GAME
Color sorting is a simple idea, but the puzzles make you think ahead. Plan your moves, use the empty tubes wisely, and find the best order to sort the balls. Ball Sort Puzzle helps you relax, focus, and give your brain a light workout at the same time. Play one quick level or relax with many in a row.

🎯 FROM EASY TO EXPERT
The first levels use only 3 colors, so anyone can learn in seconds. Every few levels a new color is added, up to 12 colors, with more tubes to plan around. If you love sort puzzles, water sort games, logic puzzles, or casual puzzle games, you will feel right at home.

💡 TIPS
• Empty tubes are your best tool. Try not to fill them too early.
• Look for tubes that are almost sorted and finish them first.
• Made a mistake? Tap Undo. Really stuck? Use +1 Tube or Restart the level.

📶 OFFLINE PUZZLE GAME
You do not need an internet connection to play. Play this offline color sort puzzle anywhere, any time.

ℹ️ GOOD TO KNOW
• Ball Sort Puzzle is free to play and shows ads. Some optional ads give you extra undos or an extra tube.
• There is no sign-up and no account.
• Privacy policy: https://offerpk.github.io/ball-sort-puzzle/privacy.html

Download Ball Sort Puzzle and start sorting! Relax, think, and enjoy a satisfying color sort puzzle on your phone.
```
